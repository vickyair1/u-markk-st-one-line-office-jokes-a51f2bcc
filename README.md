A single one-line joke about office life for the make.fun competition "One-Line Office Jokes".

> Our office fridge runs on a sign-up sheet, a cleaning rota, and one yoghurt that is now the longest-serving member of staff.

`main.py` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` and prints it to stdout.
